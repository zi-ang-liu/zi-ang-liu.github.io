Research Interests

Operations research, optimization, and machine learning — in particular, algorithms for autonomous decision-making in inventory, manufacturing, and supply chain systems. Current work covers data-driven optimization for multi-echelon supply chains under uncertainty, optimization for robotic manufacturing systems, and reinforcement learning with an emphasis on explainability and human-centric, trustworthy decision support.
