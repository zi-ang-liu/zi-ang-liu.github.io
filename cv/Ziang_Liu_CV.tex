\section{Research Interests}

\begin{cvlist}
\item[] Operations research, optimization, and machine learning---in
particular, algorithms for autonomous decision-making in inventory,
manufacturing, and supply chain systems. Current work covers data-driven
optimization for multi-echelon supply chains under uncertainty, optimization for
robotic manufacturing systems, and reinforcement learning with an emphasis on
explainability and human-centric, trustworthy decision support.
\end{cvlist}
